How to Calculate GPA at Mehran University (MUET)

Calculating your Grade Point Average (GPA) for a semester at Mehran University of Engineering and Technology (MUET) is simple. Follow these steps:

Step-by-Step GPA Calculation:

  1. Find Grade Points: Check the official MUET grading scale to match your grade to points (e.g. A+ = 4.0, A = 3.5, B+ = 3.0, etc.).
  2. Calculate Quality Points: For each course, multiply the course credit hours by the grade points earned. For example, if you got an A (3.5 points) in a 3-credit course, the quality points are: 3 × 3.5 = 10.5.
  3. Sum Up: Add all the quality points of the courses together, and add all the credit hours together.
  4. Divide: Divide the total quality points by the total credit hours. (e.g. Total Quality Points / Total Credit Hours = Semester GPA).
Calculation Example:
• Course 1 (Theory 3 credits, Grade A) → 3 × 3.5 = 10.5 quality points
• Course 2 (Theory 3 credits, Grade B+) → 3 × 3.0 = 9.0 quality points
• Course 3 (Theory 2 credits, Grade A+) → 2 × 4.0 = 8.0 quality points
• Total Credit Hours = 8
• Total Quality Points = 10.5 + 9.0 + 8.0 = 27.5
• GPA = 27.5 / 8 = 3.44

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What is a passing GPA at MUET? A: To pass a subject at MUET, a student must obtain at least a C- grade (50% marks, equivalent to 1.0 grade point). An overall GPA of 2.0 or above is generally required to maintain good academic standing.
Q: Are withdrawal (W) and incomplete (I) grades calculated? A: No. Any course marked with W (Withdrawal) or I (Incomplete) does not contribute credit hours or grade points to the GPA calculation.
Q: What is the difference between GPA and CGPA? A: GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated for a single semester, whereas CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the cumulative average of all semesters completed so far.

Glossary of Terms

Credit Hour (Cr. Hr.)
A unit of measure representing the academic weight of a course. Typically, theory lectures are 2-3 credit hours, and labs are 1 credit hour.
Grade Point (GP)
The numerical value assigned to an alphabetical grade (e.g. A = 3.5, B = 2.5, F = 0.0).
Quality Points (QP)
The product of credit hours and grade points for a specific course (Credit Hours × Grade Points).

Q: How are batch rankings determined? A: Batch rankings are calculated using a dense ranking algorithm based on cumulative CGPA. How we handle academic data and user privacy

At the MUET Results Portal, we prioritize student privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, parsing practices, and search mechanics.

Data Sourcing & Storage

All student academic records (GPA, CGPA, ranks, grades) displayed on this portal are parsed directly from public result announcements issued by the Mehran University Examination Department.

Academic Rankings

Batch rankings are calculated using a dense ranking algorithm based on cumulative CGPA.

Students with identical CGPAs receive the same rank.

For example:
Student A — CGPA 4.00 → Rank #1
Student B — CGPA 4.00 → Rank #1
Student C — CGPA 3.95 → Rank #2

This ensures rankings remain fair and accurate.
